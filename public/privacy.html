Privacy and optional analytics

Crayon Breath works without optional analytics. If you opt in, we store first-party product analytics to improve game flow and reliability.

Optional analytics can include your chosen display name, random browser identifier, random session identifier, browser language and timezone, coarse country or region supplied by a trusted edge proxy, and game activity. We use this to count consenting browsers, first-observed and returning browsers, and exact-day return rates. These are samples of connected browsers, not counts of all visitors or verified people. We do not record page views before you connect to play.

Independently of consent, anonymous operational totals record lobby creation and closure, mode, participant counts, configured and completed rounds, game outcomes, and rejected actions. Bounded server samples include process CPU, memory, event-loop lag, sockets and rooms. These operational totals do not contain player identity or location.

Game-start notifications use ntfy.sh topic crayon_breath. This topic is publicly readable. Messages contain only aggregate game mode, participant count, and configured rounds. Notifications never include display names, location, raw IP addresses, room codes, browser identifiers, chat, tokens, or drawings.

Display names are not verified identities. Location is approximate, may be wrong because of VPNs or network routing, and can be unavailable. We do not request GPS, store raw IP addresses, fingerprint devices, store chat, guessed words, drawing strokes, or full URLs.

The live analytics store is size-bounded to approximately 16 MiB, with product records retained for up to 90 days and service samples for up to 7 days. Expired records disappear from reports immediately and are removed from disk on startup and hourly while the app runs; suspended machines clean up on their next start. Older data can disappear sooner when size limits are reached. Access-controlled daily backups and volume snapshots are retained for up to 30 days, so deleted or expired records can remain in backups until those backups expire. ntfy.sh controls retention of its aggregate notifications.

Analytics failure never affects play. Contact the site owner to request deletion of analytics linked to a display name; names can be shared, so deletion may require extra information. Changing the analytics checkbox affects future connections, not records already collected.

Do not enter personal information as your display name. Children should ask a parent or guardian before opting in. This notice may change before advertising or third-party analytics starts.
