Privacy and gameplay analytics

Crayon Breath records first-party gameplay analytics for everyone who plays. It is always on and has no opt-out setting. We use it to see how the game is used and to fix problems. There are no ads or third-party trackers. The only browser storage used is one random identifier in this browser's local storage (not a cookie).

Analytics records can include your chosen display name, a random browser identifier that persists across visits, a random per-connection session identifier, browser language and timezone, coarse country or region supplied by a trusted edge proxy when available, and game activity. We use this to count browsers, first-observed and returning browsers, and exact-day return rates. Records are linked by these identifiers, so this is not anonymous data, and display names are not verified identities. We do not record page views before you connect to play.

Anonymous operational totals record lobby creation and closure, mode, participant counts, configured and completed rounds, game outcomes, and rejected actions. Bounded server samples include process CPU, memory, event-loop lag, sockets and rooms. These service samples do not contain player identity or location.

Game-start notifications use ntfy.sh topic crayon_breath. This topic is publicly readable. Messages contain only aggregate game mode, participant count, and configured rounds. Notifications never include display names, location, raw IP addresses, room codes, browser identifiers, chat, tokens, or drawings.

Display names are not verified identities. We do not request GPS, store raw IP addresses, fingerprint devices, store chat, guessed words, drawing strokes, or full URLs.

The live analytics store is size-bounded to approximately 16 MiB, with product records retained for up to 90 days and service samples for up to 7 days. Expired records disappear from reports immediately and are removed from disk on startup and hourly while the app runs; suspended machines clean up on their next start. Older data can disappear sooner when size limits are reached. Access-controlled daily backups and volume snapshots are retained for up to 30 days, so deleted or expired records can remain in backups until those backups expire. ntfy.sh controls retention of its aggregate notifications.

Do not enter personal information as your display name. Children should ask a parent or guardian before playing; a parent can contact the site owner with questions about what is recorded. We have not made a legal determination about consent requirements in every region. This notice may change before advertising or third-party analytics starts.
